<p style="margin:0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px;font:16.0px 'PingFang SC';color:#000000"><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">最简单和常见的数学归纳法是证明当</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px"> $n$ </span><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">等于任意一个自然数时某命题成立。证明分下面两步：</span></p>

</span></p>
<p style="margin:0.0px 0.0px 0.0px 0.0px;font:16.0px 'PingFang SC';color:#000000"><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px"><span>  </span></span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px;color:#0000ff">-</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px"> </span><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">假设</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px"> $n=m$ </span><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">时命题成立，那么可以</span><span style="font-family:'.SFNS-Bold';font-weight:bold;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px;color:#0000ff">**</span><span style="font-family:'.PingFangSC-Semibold';font-weight:bold;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">推导</span><span style="font-family:'.SFNS-Bold';font-weight:bold;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px;color:#0000ff">**</span><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">出在</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px"> $n=m</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px;color:#0000ff">+</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">1$ </span><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">时命题也成立。（</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">$m$ </span><span style="font-family:'PingFangSC-Regular';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px">代表任意自然数）</span><span style="font-family:'Helvetica';font-weight:normal;font-style:normal;font-size:16.00px"><br>
